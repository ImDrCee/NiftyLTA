    How a stock is read

    1. The yardstick

      Monthly candles, Bollinger bands over 20 months at 2 standard deviations. Upper and lower band are the ceiling and the floor.

    2. The trendline

      A 9 month average of the close. It tells you which way the stock has been leaning and how far the price has stretched from it.

    3. A touch

      Any month whose high reaches the ceiling or whose low reaches the floor. Months that touch close together are one episode.

    4. Is the price justified

      For each past episode, take the earnings known at that time and compare them with the latest quarter. Banks and lenders use net interest income and profit instead of sales. One-off other income is replaced by the stock's own typical level.

      What this cannot tell you

      • Screener gives 13 quarters, so earnings context exists only for touches from about August 2023 onward. Older touches show no earnings.
      • October 2026 is a partial month. Its candle will change until the month ends.
      • Core earnings are an estimate: other income is replaced by the stock's own median and tax is set at 25%. Banks use pre-tax profit as reported.
      • Each stock has one to four episodes per side. That is a pattern, not a statistic.
      • Prices come from Zerodha Kite daily candles and are split and bonus adjusted. Fundamentals come from Screener consolidated results.
